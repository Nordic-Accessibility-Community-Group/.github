# Code of Conduct

The Nordic Accessibility Community Group is a neutral space for sharing practical experience, comparing approaches across countries, and learning from people working with accessibility in different roles and sectors.

People may participate as individuals or as representatives of organizations. Participation should support the group's discussions and activities. Advertising, product promotion, and sales-driven contributions are not welcome.

This code of conduct complements the [W3C Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct](https://www.w3.org/Consortium/cepc/).

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but listen when someone says something is harmful or exclusionary.
- Make room for different experience levels, roles, countries, disabilities, and perspectives.
- Be careful when discussing disability, accessibility barriers, and lived experience.
- Use clear language and explain abbreviations when needed.
- Support factual claims with sources where possible.
- Keep discussions focused on accessibility, standards, legislation, implementation, monitoring, education, and related community work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Product promotion, sales pitches, or repeated self-promotion.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or ignoring moderator requests.
- Dismissing accessibility needs or lived experience in a hostile or disrespectful way.

## If something happens

If you experience or notice behavior that does not fit these expectations, contact the repository maintainers or the W3C Community Group chairs through the group's usual channels.

Maintainers or chairs may ask someone to change their behavior, edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or take other steps needed to keep the space useful and respectful.
