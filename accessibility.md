The W3C Nordic Accessibility Community Group exists to increase accessibility. So we're of course interested of hearing about accessibility barriers.

## Code of conduct
We have a [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), please refer to it. In summary-  be a nice person and don't assume ill intent.

## Contributing
We are very happy for improvement suggestions, so if you encounter something that makes your experience a bit sad, please let us know. [How to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md).

- Added langhref attributes to links.
