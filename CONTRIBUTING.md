# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve the Nordic Accessibility Community Group resources.

This GitHub organization is used for practical, community-maintained information about digital accessibility, accessibility standards, European accessibility requirements, monitoring agencies, templates, learning opportunities, and resources for staying up to date.

Small contributions are welcome. A missing link, a corrected authority name, or clearer wording can be useful.

## How to contribute

You can contribute by:

- opening an issue with a question, correction, or suggested source
- opening a pull request with a concrete edit
- joining discussion through the [W3C community group](https://www.w3.org/community/nordic-accessibility/) when a topic needs wider input

## What makes a good contribution

When adding or changing factual information:

- prefer official or primary sources
- include the source link close to the claim
- include dates when the status may change, such as laws, standards, enforcement, or monitoring activity
- separate confirmed facts from interpretation or opinion
- say when something is uncertain

When editing text:

- keep it clear, concise, and practical
- do not present contributions as legal advice
- avoid vendor promotion, sales language, or product marketing
- preserve the existing structure unless there is a reason to change it

## Accessibility and clarity

Please write in a way that is easy to scan and understand:

- use headings and lists where they help
- use descriptive link text
- do not rely on screenshots alone
- explain abbreviations when they may not be obvious
- avoid unnecessary jargon

## Privacy and sensitive information

Do not add private information, confidential material, passwords, unpublished meeting details, or personal contact details unless they are already intended to be public.
